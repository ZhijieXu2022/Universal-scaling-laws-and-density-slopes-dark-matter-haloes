# Universal scaling laws and density slopes for dark matter haloes

Z. Xu (2023), *Universal scaling laws and density slopes for dark matter haloes*, Scientific Reports **13**, 4165, [doi:10.1038/s41598-023-31083-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-31083-z); arXiv: [2209.03313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03313).

This repository holds the manuscript and the plotted data behind the figures and tables of the paper. The same files are archived on Zenodo: [doi:10.5281/zenodo.23989405](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23989405) ([https://zenodo.org/records/23989405](https://zenodo.org/records/23989405)).

## Major findings (cosmological N-body simulation and galaxy rotation curves):

- A constant energy cascade rate ε<sub>u</sub> ≈ −4.6×10<sup>−7</sup> m²/s³ acts on dark matter flow from small to large scales, giving the two-thirds law v<sub>r</sub>² ∝ (ε<sub>u</sub> r)<sup>2/3</sup>, which is confirmed by N-body simulations.
- Equivalently, the halo density inside the scale radius follows the four-thirds law ρ<sub>s</sub> ∝ ε<sub>u</sub><sup>2/3</sup> G<sup>−1</sup> r<sub>s</sub><sup>−4/3</sup>, which is confirmed by galaxy rotation curves.
- The largest halo scale is r<sub>l</sub> ≈ −u<sub>0</sub><sup>3</sup>/ε<sub>u</sub>, where u<sub>0</sub> is the velocity dispersion.
- For collisionless dark matter, the smallest scale is r<sub>η</sub> ∝ (−Għ/ε<sub>u</sub>)<sup>1/3</sup> ≈ 10<sup>−13</sup> m, with mass scale m<sub>X</sub> ∝ (−ε<sub>u</sub>ħ<sup>5</sup>G<sup>−4</sup>)<sup>1/9</sup> ≈ 10<sup>12</sup> GeV.
- For self-interacting dark matter, r<sub>η</sub> ∝ ε<sub>u</sub><sup>2</sup> G<sup>−3</sup>(σ/m)<sup>3</sup>, where σ/m is the cross section of interaction.
- Gravity-dominant region has asymptotic density slope is γ = −4/3;
